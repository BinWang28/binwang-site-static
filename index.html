About

I'm an AI research scientist at Apodex in Singapore, where I'm the main contributor to our pretraining data acquisition pipeline. Before that, I built Apodex's deep-research agent harness and its evaluation framework.

Earlier, at A*STAR, I was a lead contributor to MERaLiON, Singapore's national audio LLM, and built the AudioBench and SeaEval benchmarks. I received my Ph.D. from USC in 2021.
