Scientist

Institute for Infocomm Research (I²R), A*STAR, Singapore

Audio-language models and multilingual evaluation

At A*STAR I²R, I was a lead contributor to MERaLiON AudioLLM, covering data preparation, evaluation, and training on hundreds of H100 GPUs in a single run. The work was part of Singapore’s National Multimodal LLM Programme.

  • AudioBench: led an audio-language benchmark spanning eight tasks and 26 datasets. First author, NAACL 2025.
  • SeaEval: led evaluation of cross-lingual consistency and cultural reasoning. Co-first author, NAACL 2024.
  • Singlish speech data: led curation of 10,000 hours of Singlish speech for training, with a subset publicly released.

I also worked on dialogue summarization and collaborated on multilingual instruction tuning and multimodal evaluation.
