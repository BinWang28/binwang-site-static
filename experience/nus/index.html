Research Fellow

National University of Singapore (NUS) · Supervisor: Prof. Haizhou Li (IEEE Fellow)

Dialogue summarization and NLP evaluation

I joined NUS in September 2021 after completing my Ph.D. at USC, working with Prof. Haizhou Li.

  • FacEval: developed evaluation of factual consistency in dialogue summarization, releasing annotated data and an evaluation toolkit. First author, EMNLP 2022.
  • EvalRank: proposed a method for evaluating word and sentence representations, testing its alignment with downstream performance across more than 60 models. First author, ACL 2022.
  • InstructDS: built a dialogue summarizer tuned on synthesized query-based instruction data. First author, EMNLP 2023.

This work connected representation learning with the practical question of how to tell whether a model’s output preserves meaning.
